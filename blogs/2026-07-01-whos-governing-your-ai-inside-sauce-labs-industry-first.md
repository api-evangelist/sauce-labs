Sauce Labs became the first software quality platform to achieve ISO/IEC 42001 certification, an international standard for responsible AI governance. The third-party verified certification covers AI capabilities like Sauce AI for Insights and Test Authoring, demonstrating rigorous controls around risk, transparency, and human oversight.
